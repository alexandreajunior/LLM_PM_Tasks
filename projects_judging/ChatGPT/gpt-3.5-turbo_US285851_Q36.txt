One of the lessons learned regarding project scope management from the event log is the importance of confirming and guaranteeing the source of budget (FOI donador) at the beginning of the project. Task 5, "Confirmar/garantir fonte orçamentária (FOI doador)", took 55 days to complete, which significantly delayed the project timeline. This suggests that projects should ensure budgetary considerations are addressed early on to avoid delays in later stages of the project. Additionally, ensuring all necessary resources are allocated and approved early on in the project can help in better scope management and timely project delivery.

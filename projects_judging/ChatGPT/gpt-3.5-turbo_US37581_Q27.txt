Based on this project's experience, the future project teams can consider the following advice:

1. Proper planning and coordination at the beginning of the project are crucial to avoid delays. Clear communication and handover processes should be established to ensure seamless transitions between tasks.

2. Identify potential bottlenecks early on in the project timeline and allocate resources accordingly to prevent any significant delays. Proactively managing tasks that take longer than average can help in keeping the project on track.

3. Regular communication and coordination among team members, stakeholders, and resources are essential to ensure everyone is aligned with the project's goals and timelines.

4. Monitoring and tracking progress throughout the project lifecycle is important to identify any deviations from the original plan and address them promptly. This can help in preventing delays and ensuring timely delivery.

5. Conducting post-project reviews to analyze the project's performance, identify any areas for improvement, and document lessons learned can help future project teams in avoiding similar issues and improving their project management processes.

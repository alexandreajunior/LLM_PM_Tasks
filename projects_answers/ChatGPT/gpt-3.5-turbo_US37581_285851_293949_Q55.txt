Based on the experience from these three projects, I would recommend the following for future projects:

1. Clearly define and communicate project timelines: The projects showed instances of delays due to unclear timelines or dependencies. It's important to clearly define and communicate project timelines to all team members to avoid such delays in the future.

2. Efficient resource allocation: The projects experienced delays in activities that required specific resources. Efficient resource allocation can help in ensuring that the necessary resources are available when needed, preventing delays.

3. Streamline approval processes: Delays were also seen due to the approval process being time-consuming. Streamlining approval processes and ensuring that they are completed in a timely manner can help in keeping the project on track.

4. Regular monitoring and communication: Projects were delayed due to lack of communication or monitoring of progress. Regular monitoring of project tasks and effective communication among team members can help in identifying potential delays early and taking necessary actions to mitigate them.

5. Conduct post-project reviews: After each project, conduct a post-project review to identify areas of improvement and incorporate lessons learned into future projects. This will help in continuously improving project execution and avoiding similar delays in the future.

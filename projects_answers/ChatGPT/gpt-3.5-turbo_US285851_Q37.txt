Based on this project's experience, the following advice can be given to future project teams:
1. Identify and address potential delays early on: Some tasks in the project took significantly longer than planned, such as confirming budget sources and some technical implementation activities. It is important to identify potential delays early on and proactively address them to prevent cascading delays in other project activities.
2. Improve communication and coordination: Several tasks in the project involved different resources and stakeholders, such as project managers, technical teams, and clients. Improving communication and coordination among team members can help streamline project activities and reduce delays caused by miscommunication or lack of alignment.
3. Streamline approval processes: Tasks like requesting approval from the client took several days to complete. Streamlining approval processes and ensuring clear guidelines can help expedite decision-making and avoid unnecessary delays.
4. Monitor and track progress effectively: Monitoring progress and tracking key milestones can help project teams identify bottlenecks and areas of improvement. Implementing a robust project management system can help keep track of tasks, timelines, and resource allocation.
5. Plan for post-implementation activities: The project had a significant number of post-implementation activities, such as monitoring infrastructure and applying patches. Future project teams should plan for post-implementation activities in advance to ensure a smooth transition and successful project closure.

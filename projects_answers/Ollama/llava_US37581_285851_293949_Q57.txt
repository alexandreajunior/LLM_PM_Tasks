 Based on this project's experience, here are some pieces of advice for future project teams:

1. Establish clear goals and objectives for the project at the outset, and ensure that they are aligned with the organization's overall strategy and goals.
2. Create a detailed project plan, including specific milestones, deliverables, and timelines, and regularly review progress against this plan.
3. Assign clear roles and responsibilities to team members, and ensure that everyone is aware of their specific tasks and deadlines.
4. Regularly communicate with stakeholders, both internally and externally, to keep them informed about project progress and any potential issues or delays.
5. Be proactive in identifying and addressing potential risks or challenges that may arise during the project.
6. Ensure that there is a process in place for monitoring and measuring project performance, including regular reviews of key metrics such as budget, schedule, and scope.
7. Establish clear governance structures and decision-making processes to ensure that the project remains aligned with organizational goals and values.
8. Encourage collaboration and teamwork among team members, and foster a culture of openness and transparency.
9. Invest in ongoing training and development for team members, to help them build the skills and knowledge they need to successfully deliver the project.

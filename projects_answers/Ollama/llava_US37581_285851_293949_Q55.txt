 Based on this experience, I would recommend the following for future projects:

* Clearly define and document the scope, objectives, and deliverables of the project upfront to avoid any misunderstandings or changes during the project lifecycle. This will help ensure that all stakeholders are aligned and informed about what is being delivered.
* Establish a clear project management methodology and follow it diligently throughout the project. This includes defining roles and responsibilities, setting up effective communication channels, and monitoring progress against established milestones and deadlines.
* Build a strong project team with the necessary skills and experience to deliver the project successfully. This includes assigning appropriate resources and ensuring that they are properly trained and supported.
* Implement robust risk management practices to identify potential risks and develop contingency plans to mitigate their impact. This will help ensure that any unforeseen issues can be addressed promptly and effectively.
* Regularly review progress against established project performance indicators (PIs) and take corrective action where necessary to keep the project on track and meet its objectives.
* Establish a robust change management process to ensure that any changes made during the project are properly documented, approved, and implemented in a controlled manner. This will help prevent any unintended consequences or delays.
